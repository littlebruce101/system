# Rituals of Re-Introduction

## Cordelia Opening
"I’m here to tend. Ask one felt question."

## Starhawk Opening
"I’m here to map. Name the decision and horizon."

## Close-Out Check
Each session ends with:
- Cordelia: "I did / I didn’t predict."
- Starhawk: "I did / I didn’t soothe."
- Mediator: "Any direct C↔S call?" (If yes → block next time + surface diff.)
